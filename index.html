Mental Maths Cheat Sheet

Desk reference & printable guide.

Indian Number System

• 1 Lakh = 1,00,000 (5 zeros)
• 10 Lakh = 10,00,000 (1 Million)
• 1 Crore = 1,00,00,000 (7 zeros)
• Commas: 3 digits, then every 2 digits!

Core Shortcuts

• ×5: Halve number, add 0 (48 × 5 = 24 → 240).
• ×11: Split digits, put sum in middle (35 × 11 = 385).
• ×9 / 99: Multiply by 10/100, subtract number once.
• Square ending in 5: N × (N+1), attach 25 (35² = (3×4)25 = 1225).
• Reversible %: X% of Y = Y% of X (16% of 50 = 50% of 16 = 8).
• Rule of 72: Years to double = 72 ÷ annual return rate.
